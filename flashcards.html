How the scheduling works

A simplified SM-2: each card has an ease and an interval. “Again” resets it to today, “Hard” nudges the interval up slightly, “Good” multiplies it by the ease, and “Easy” multiplies it more and raises the ease. Intervals grow 1 → 3 → 7 → 16 → 35 days, so a card you know well disappears for a month. Progress is stored in this browser only.
